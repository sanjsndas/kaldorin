    ❓ root questions

    How often should I water deep?

    Water deeply 1-2 times per week, depending on soil type. Aim for 1 inch per week, including rainfall.

    Is crop rotation necessary in small gardens?

    Absolutely. Even in raised beds, rotation prevents nutrient depletion and disrupts pest cycles.

    What’s the best mulch?

    Shredded leaves or straw are excellent; they break down slowly and feed the soil food web.
