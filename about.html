our manifesto

the wild border philosophy

by Helena Kaldorin · editor-in-chief

A border is not a line; it is a transition. We apply this to our writing, our design, and our relationship with the natural world. Kaldorin Wildthicket exists at the edge between tradition and innovation, between the wild and the cultivated. We are gardeners of both earth and language.

  • We honor the mycelial networks that connect all living things.
  • We believe that beauty is a form of function, and function can be beautiful.
  • We practice what we preach — our garden is our laboratory.

❓ about the journal

Why "Wildthicket"?

A wild thicket is a place of dense, intertwined life — exactly what we aim to cultivate: rich, layered, and full of hidden connections.

Is this a commercial blog?

No. We are ad-free and supported by our readers. We believe in the integrity of the content.

How can I contribute?

We welcome pitches from gardeners, ecologists, and storytellers. Reach out via our contact page.
