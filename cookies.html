🍪 cookies policy

We keep cookies simple, transparent, and minimal — just like our approach to everything else.

how we use cookies

Last updated: 22 July 2026 · Kaldorin Wildthicket

🌿 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us understand how you interact with our content — but we keep it light and respectful.

✨ We use only essential and performance cookies. No tracking, no advertising, no third-party data sharing.

📋 2. Cookies we use

We use a minimal set of cookies to keep the site running smoothly and to understand which pages are most loved by our readers.

NamePurposeDuration
wildthicket_sessionMaintains your session and preferences (e.g., dark/light mode)Session
gardener_analyticsAnonymised page view counts — no personal data30 days
consent_givenRemembers your cookie preference (so we don't ask again)1 year

We do not use any advertising, tracking, or cross-site cookies.

⚙️ 3. Your control

You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. However, please note that disabling essential cookies may affect your experience.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Site permissions → Cookies and site data

We also provide a simple consent banner when you first visit — you can change your mind at any time.

🔗 4. Third-party services

We use a privacy-first analytics tool that does not use cookies for tracking. Our email hosting and contact forms do not set any tracking cookies.

We do not use Google Analytics, social media pixels, or ad networks.

📜 5. Updates to this policy

We will update this page if our cookie practices change. The latest version is always available here. Significant changes will be announced on our homepage or via newsletter.
