🔒 privacy policy

We treat your data like a delicate seedling — with care, transparency, and respect. This policy explains how we handle information at Kaldorin Wildthicket.

your data, our commitment

Last updated: 22 July 2026 · Kaldorin Wildthicket

🌱 1. Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to share. This includes:

  • Contact details — name, email address, and any message you send via forms.
  • Garden correspondence — letters, questions, or stories you share with us.
  • Usage data — anonymised analytics to understand which pages are most loved.

✨ We never sell or rent your data. Your information stays within the Wildthicket grounds.

📬 2. How we use your information

  • To reply — we respond to your letters, questions, and collaborations.
  • To improve — we use anonymised data to understand what our readers enjoy.
  • To send occasional updates — only if you opt in, and always with a way to unsubscribe.

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

🍃 3. Data storage & security

We store your data on secure servers with industry-standard encryption. Access is limited to essential staff. We retain correspondence for as long as needed to serve you, and delete it upon request.

  • 📧 Emails are stored securely and deleted after 3 years of inactivity.
  • 📁 Form submissions are not stored on our website — they are sent directly to our private mailbox.

🔗 4. Third-party services

We use minimal third-party tools:

  • Email hosting — for sending and receiving messages.
  • Analytics — privacy-focused, no cookies, no tracking across sites.

We do not use social media tracking pixels or ad networks.

🧾 5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, simply send us a letter — we’ll reply within 2 days.

📜 6. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change. The latest version is always available here. Significant changes will be announced via our newsletter or on the homepage.
